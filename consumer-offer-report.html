Consumer Offer Report

Designing a transparent vehicle appraisal experience that bridges the trust gap between dealers and consumers during trade-in negotiations.

vAuto / Cox Automotive

Role: UX Designer

What it is

A consumer-facing report that shows how a dealer's trade-in offer was calculated.

The Consumer Offer Report is a transparency tool within vAuto's Provision suite. When a dealer appraises a vehicle, the report breaks down the offer into clear components — vehicle valuation, condition adjustments, and repair costs — so the consumer can see exactly how the number was reached.

It serves both sides: consumers get the trust and clarity they need to feel confident in the offer, and dealers reduce missed trades by removing the black-box perception of appraisals.

Why it matters

Missed trades cost dealers real money. Transparency closes the gap.

Trade-in negotiations are one of the highest-friction moments in the car buying process. Consumers often distrust dealer offers because they can't see the reasoning behind them. The Consumer Offer Report makes the appraisal logic visible — turning a negotiation into a conversation.

This project sits at the intersection of consumer-facing UX and dealer tools, requiring design that serves two very different audiences with one artifact.
